# 🚦 City-Wide ANPR & Traffic Intelligence

> **SIH 2026 — Smart Multi-Camera Vehicle Tracking & Traffic Analytics**

A centralized AI-powered platform designed to connect city-wide **ANPR/CCTV camera networks** and turn isolated camera data into actionable traffic intelligence.

### 🎯 Problem

Current ANPR systems largely operate in isolated silos, making it difficult to:

* Accurately recognize plates in real-world conditions
* Track a vehicle across multiple camera locations
* Understand city-wide traffic movement and congestion
* Detect blacklisted vehicles or suspicious route patterns
